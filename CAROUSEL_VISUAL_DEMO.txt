USER INTERACTION FLOWS:

1️⃣  CLICK NEXT ARROW (▶)
    Daily (Index: 0) → Weekly (Index: 1)
    Animation: Current slide fades left → New slide fades in from right

2️⃣  CLICK PREVIOUS ARROW (◀)
    Daily (Index: 0) → Monthly (Index: 2, wraps around)
    Animation: Current slide fades right → New slide fades in from left

3️⃣  CLICK DOT (Weekly)
    Any Index → Weekly (Index: 1)
    Animation: Instant slide to selected view
    ◀  [Daily] [✓Weekly] [Monthly]  ▶

4️⃣  SEQUENTIAL NAVIGATION
    Daily (0) → Click Next → Weekly (1)
    Weekly (1) → Click Next → Monthly (2)
    Monthly (2) → Click Next → Daily (0) [wraps around]
